# Publishing GPT Brief

## App Identity

- App name: `Полиглот Болгария`
- Platform: Android
- Category: Education / Language Learning
- Main goal: help Russian-speaking and Ukrainian-speaking learners study Bulgarian through structured lessons, fixed phrase practice, and vocabulary review

## Core Product Summary

`Полиглот Болгария` is a Bulgarian language learning app built around a clear lesson route and practical everyday phrases. The app teaches through textbook-aligned lessons, sentence-building exercises, theory blocks, and a built-in dictionary with flashcard-style review.

The course is designed to feel structured rather than random:

- each lesson follows a topic
- exercises use curated fixed phrases, not randomly generated sentences
- learners see theory, then practice, then progress and results

## Target Audience

- Russian-speaking learners studying Bulgarian
- Ukrainian-speaking learners studying Bulgarian
- beginners and early independent learners
- users who want a practical course with everyday topics rather than abstract grammar drills
- learners who want to study through a mobile trainer alongside a textbook

## Current Course Structure

The app currently includes 11 lessons aligned to practical beginner topics:

1. Greeting and introduction
2. Food and breakfast
3. Restaurant
4. Shopping
5. City, address, and purchases
6. Family
7. Weather and time
8. Clothes and colors
9. Home and furniture
10. Transport
11. Daily routine

## Learning Method

The app is based on:

- short theory sections for each lesson
- fixed phrase exercises
- sentence builder mechanics
- progress tracking
- lesson unlocking by earned score
- vocabulary review through a dictionary and flashcards

Important pedagogical point:

- the app does not rely on random sentence generation
- phrase pools are curated and aligned with the lesson topic
